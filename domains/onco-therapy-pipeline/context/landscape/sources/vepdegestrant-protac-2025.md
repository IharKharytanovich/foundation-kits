# Vepdegestrant (ARV-471) — PROTAC Reaching Phase 3

A PROTAC (PROteolysis-TArgeting Chimera) is a bifunctional small molecule with one warhead binding a target protein and another recruiting an E3 ubiquitin ligase, tagging the target for proteasomal destruction. Unlike an inhibitor that must occupy an active site, a degrader acts **catalytically and event-driven** — it can eliminate "undruggable" and scaffold proteins that lack a functional pocket.

## Vepdegestrant

- **First PROTAC ever evaluated in a phase 3 clinical trial** (VERITAC-2), degrading the **estrogen receptor (ER)** in ER+/HER2- advanced breast cancer.
- Oral, cereblon-recruiting degrader (Arvinas / Pfizer).
- **FDA accepted a New Drug Application on August 8, 2025** for ESR1-mutated ER+/HER2- advanced breast cancer — the first regulatory submission for the modality.
- Robust ER degradation and anti-tumor activity with a favorable oral safety profile in earlier cohorts; the phase 3 benefit was concentrated in the ESR1-mutant subgroup.

## Modality Maturity

- **Zero PROTAC degraders are FDA-approved** as of mid-2025; >40 degraders (PROTAC + molecular glue) are in clinical trials (ARV-110/bavdegalutamide in prostate cancer, BMS/CFT programs, KT-333/KT-413).
- Maturity: **clinical, not approved**; personalization type **off-the-shelf** (biomarker-matched by ESR1 mutation status, not patient-manufactured).

## Significance

Vepdegestrant is the modality's proof-of-clinical-viability inflection: it moves targeted protein degradation from a chemistry platform to a late-stage therapeutic class and defines the ESR1-mutant, endocrine-resistant setting as the beachhead indication.
