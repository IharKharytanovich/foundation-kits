# FDA Approval — Afamitresgene Autoleucel (Tecelra), 2024

On August 1, 2024 the FDA granted **accelerated approval** to afamitresgene autoleucel (Tecelra, Adaptimmune) for unresectable or metastatic **synovial sarcoma** in HLA-A*02-positive adults whose tumors express **MAGE-A4**, after prior chemotherapy. It is the **first engineered T-cell receptor (TCR) therapy** approved for a solid tumor.

## Modality and Mechanism

Afami-cel is an **autologous, bespoke cell product**: a patient's T cells are transduced ex vivo with an affinity-enhanced TCR recognizing a MAGE-A4 peptide presented on HLA-A*02. Because a TCR reads intracellular antigens via HLA (unlike CAR-T, which needs a surface target), it can address the many solid-tumor antigens that are not cell-surface proteins. It is HLA-restricted, so eligibility is gated by both HLA type and antigen expression.

## Key Trial Data (SPEARHEAD-1)

- **Overall response rate ~43%** in the pivotal cohort (phase 2); phase 1 dose-escalation ORR was 24%.
- **Median time to response ~4.9 weeks**; median duration of response ~6 months.
- Manufacturing is a multi-week ex vivo cycle with per-patient lot release.

## Significance

Tecelra proves TCR-T can reach a solid tumor and expands the addressable antigen space beyond surface targets. Together with lifileucel (TIL, Feb 2024) and tarlatamab (BiTE, May 2024), it marks 2024 as the year T-cell-redirecting modalities crossed into solid tumors.
