# VISION Trial — 177Lu-PSMA-617 (Pluvicto)

Radioligand (radiopharmaceutical) therapy delivers a cytotoxic radioisotope to tumor cells via a targeting ligand that binds a tumor-associated receptor, irradiating the tumor from within (short-range beta or alpha emission).

## Pluvicto / VISION

- **Agent**: lutetium Lu-177 vipivotide tetraxetan (177Lu-PSMA-617) — a PSMA-targeting small-molecule ligand chelated to the beta-emitter lutetium-177.
- **VISION trial** (Sartor et al., NEJM 2021): in PSMA-positive metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) after taxane + ARPI, adding 177Lu-PSMA-617 to standard care **significantly prolonged overall survival** (median 15.3 vs 11.3 months) and **radiographic progression-free survival** (8.7 vs 3.4 months).
- **FDA approval** March 2022; **indication expanded March 28, 2025** to include patients before chemotherapy (post-ARPI).

## Theranostic Principle

The same PSMA ligand can carry a **diagnostic** isotope (68Ga/18F-PSMA PET) to select patients and confirm target expression, then a **therapeutic** isotope (177Lu) to treat — a "see-it, treat-it" theranostic pair. This makes radioligand therapy **matched** (companion-imaging-selected), not bespoke.

## Class Context

The predecessor **177Lu-DOTATATE (Lutathera)** was approved in 2018 for somatostatin-receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumors. Alpha-emitter programs (225Ac-PSMA) and new targets (FAP, SSTR, GRPR) are in trials. Radioligands reach **disseminated, radiographically PSMA-avid** disease systemically — a delivery route unavailable to solid-tumor gene editing.
