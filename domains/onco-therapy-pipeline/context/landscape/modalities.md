# Modality Landscape

<!-- @anchor: approved-backbone -->
## Approved Backbone (matched / off-the-shelf)

Targeted small molecules (largest class), checkpoint inhibitors, ADC (~15 by 2025), bispecifics/BiTE, radioligands (Lutathera 2018, Pluvicto 2022), synthetic lethality (PARP approved; PRMT5/WRN clinical), tumor-agnostic regimens (~8).

<!-- @anchor: bespoke-2024 -->
## Bespoke N-of-1 — 2024 Breakthroughs

[[adoptive-cell-therapy]]: lifileucel/Amtagvi (TIL, melanoma, ORR 31.5%), afami-cel/Tecelra (TCR-T, synovial sarcoma, ORR 43.2%). [[neoantigen-vaccine]]: mRNA-4157 (up to 34 neoepitopes, KEYNOTE-942 −44% recurrence risk), autogene cevumeran (up to 20, PDAC). tarlatamab/Imdelltra (DLL3×CD3 BiTE, SCLC, ORR 40%).

<!-- @anchor: frontier -->
## Frontier (clinical / preclinical)

PROTAC/molecular glue (>40 clinical, 0 approved), [[genome-editing-modality]] (ex vivo clinical; in-vivo tumor preclinical), RNA therapeutics (few in onco), oncolytic virotherapy (T-VEC 2015; RP1 decision ~2026), microbiome (Ph1–2), AI design (enabler, no approved onco drug yet).

The fastest personalized loop today runs through [[neoantigen-vaccine]], not direct DNA targeting.
