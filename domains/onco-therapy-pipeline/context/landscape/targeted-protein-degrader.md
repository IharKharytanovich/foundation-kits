# Targeted Protein Degraders (PROTAC)

[[targeted-protein-degrader]] is a bifunctional small molecule: one warhead binds the target protein, the other recruits an E3 ubiquitin ligase, tagging the target for proteasomal destruction. Because it acts **catalytically and event-driven** — recycled after each degradation event rather than occupying an active site — it can eliminate scaffold and "undruggable" proteins that classical inhibitors cannot touch.

**Maturity: clinical (not approved). Personalization type: off-the-shelf** (biomarker-matched).

## The Vepdegestrant Inflection

**Vepdegestrant (ARV-471)** is the **first PROTAC ever evaluated in a phase 3 trial** (VERITAC-2), degrading the estrogen receptor in ER+/HER2- advanced breast cancer. The **FDA accepted its New Drug Application on August 8, 2025** for the ESR1-mutated subgroup — the modality's first regulatory submission (see `sources/vepdegestrant-protac-2025.md`).

## Maturity Reality

- **Zero targeted protein degraders are FDA-approved** as of mid-2025.
- **>40 degraders** (PROTAC + molecular glue) are in clinical trials — ARV-110/bavdegalutamide (androgen receptor, prostate), and cereblon-recruiting programs in heme and solid tumors.
- The class is off-the-shelf and orally dosed; patients are selected by driver-dependence biomarkers (ESR1 mutation, AR amplification), not per-patient manufacture.

## Frontier Position

Degraders sharpen the pharmacology of the approved small-molecule backbone: where an allele-specific inhibitor must occupy a mutant pocket, a degrader can remove the whole protein, potentially reaching resistance mutations that evade occupancy-based drugs. Vepdegestrant is the proof that the modality can clear late-stage trials; broad approval remains the open milestone.

refines:: [[genome-editing-modality]]

Both degraders and genome editing aim to remove a driver's function rather than merely inhibit it — the degrader does so reversibly at the protein level with an off-the-shelf oral drug, whereas [[genome-editing-modality]] acts irreversibly at the DNA level but is gated by the solid-tumor delivery wall.
