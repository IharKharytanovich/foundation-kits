# End-to-End Personalized Onco-Therapy Pipeline — Knowledge Base

Your starting point for reasoning about the whole personalized cancer-therapy process as one **evolving** map, across both design branches — immune (neoantigen vaccines, CAR-T/TCR-T/TIL, BiTE/ICI) and genetic (oncolytic virus, CRISPR/base/prime, ASO/siRNA). The discipline throughout: **separate what you can compute from what is wet-lab / clinical, and grade every number's provenance.** A broad map is only useful if each node says how much to trust it.

The pipeline shape and its binding constraint live in [map.md](map.md) — a living file that changes as the research develops. Trust it over any order implied by folder names. Today's binding wall is **delivery into the solid tumor**.

- [delivery/](delivery/index.md): How the payload physically reaches the target cell in vivo: LNP vs AAV vs virion vs cell carriers, biodistribution, and penetration of the tumor microenvironment. This is the pipeline's binding constraint — median 0.7% of dose reaches a solid tumor, 1–2% escapes the endosome. The 2024–26 wins bypass the wall (aim at the liver, inject locally, hit blood cells) rather than break it. Covers LNP vs AAV tradeoffs, ionizable lipid design (computable pKa vs empirical escape), intratumoral injection (83–86% CR in mice), and in-vivo CAR-T (~90% CD8+ transfection via DARPin-LNP). Raw sources in `sources/`. (7 files)
- [design/](design/index.md): Turning the diff into a therapeutic via a cycle over two branches with different evaluability and economics. - [diff/](diff/index.md): The tumor-normal difference across nine analytical layers: (pre)alignment → somatic variant calling (SNV/indel/CNV/SV) → copy number/ploidy/WGD + HLA-LOH → clonality → TMB/MSI + mutational signatures/HRD → HLA typing → neoantigen prediction → non-canonical (splicing/fusion/ERV) discovery → immunogenicity modelling. Computable: alignment, calling, TMB/MSI, HLA, CN/purity/ploidy, signature attribution, MHC-binding/presentation (PPV≈0.83). Empirical / not computable de novo: neoantigen immunogenicity and TCR recognition (near-random AUC 0.52–0.60; see [walls/index.md](../walls/index.md)); unstable: SV calling, subclone inference; purity-gated: all copy-number calls. - [efficacy/](efficacy/index.md): Two kinds of metric plus the loop back to diff. Delivery metrics (editing %, %CAR+, expression) and action metrics (RECIST, MRD/ctDNA) are countable — computable. But the link metric → clinical outcome (surrogacy) is empirical and unsettled: ctDNA is prognostic (HR 5–11 for recurrence) but not an accepted OS/DFS surrogate (no trial-level R² >0.7); in-vivo editing measurement in solid tumors remains aspirational (no circulating readout, biopsy is one spatial sample). Resistance/evolution closes the cycle: pre-existing antigen-negative subclones (30–60% of CAR-T relapses, detectable at 0.003–1.8% VAF before treatment) drive relapse and feed a new diff (see [map.md](../map.md)). - [manufacturing/](manufacturing/index.md): Production of the therapeutic: mRNA/LNP (cell-free, the only truly N-of-1-scalable platform) vs viral vector vs cell product, then titre, yield, purification, QC/release. - [nonviral-delivery/](nonviral-delivery/index.md): The platform frontier for getting a payload past the delivery wall without a replicating virus. Every systemic lipid nanoparticle defaults to the liver (ApoE→LDLR, ~90% of dose), so this cluster holds the strategies that redirect or route around that default: **charge-based organ retargeting** (SORT lipids dial tropism to lung or spleen), **ligand-conjugated LNPs** (antibody/DARPin-decorated particles that program T cells, hematopoietic stem cells, or endothelium — the LNP route to in-vivo CAR-T), **biological carriers** (CD47-shielded exosomes, cell-membrane-cloaked nanoparticles, and hypoxia-seeking engineered bacteria that colonize the tumor core drugs can't reach), and **physical/local delivery** (focused ultrasound + microbubbles opening the blood-brain barrier, and injectable hydrogel depots that place immunostimulants directly in the tumor). Each is scored honestly: the strongest quantitative wins are extrahepatic organs, immune cells, and local depots — direct systemic solid-tumor-parenchyma targeting remains the least mature, and several headline platforms (iExosomes, membrane-coated NPs, hydrogel immunotherapy) are clinically unproven or preclinical. This cluster extends the domain's [delivery-bypass](../delivery/bypass.md) thesis: the wins route around the wall, they do not break it. Raw per-paper breakdowns in `sources/`. (8 files)

- [tme-barriers/](tme-barriers/index.md): The tumor microenvironment blockers that sit *downstream* of delivery: even when a virus, a nanoparticle, or a T cell reaches the tumor, a stacked set of physical, vascular, metabolic, and cell-intrinsic barriers neutralizes it. This cluster holds the two-gate failure of solid-tumor immunotherapy — first the effector T cells cannot get *in* (chemokine-axis mismatch, an anergic/killing endothelium, a dense desmoplastic stroma, high interstitial fluid pressure), then, if they arrive, they cannot *function* (immunosuppressive Treg/MDSC/TAM, a metabolically hostile acidic-hypoxic-nutrient-starved milieu, epigenetically locked exhaustion) — plus antigen escape that defeats the response over time. The same physical barriers (stroma, interstitial pressure) are the biological basis of the pipeline's binding [solid-tumor delivery wall](../delivery/delivery-wall.md), so this cluster explains *why* that wall exists. It also holds the bypass attempts — vascular normalization + high-endothelial-venule induction (the most promising), and stroma modification (mostly failed in phase 3) — and marks each claim computable-vs-empirical with graded provenance. Raw per-paper breakdowns in `sources/`. (12 files)
- [viral-delivery/](viral-delivery/index.md): How replicating oncolytic viruses and engineered gene/cell vectors physically reach cancer, what blocks them, and how the 2023–2026 field routes around those blocks. The binding constraint here is **systemic delivery of a replication-competent virus**: neutralizing antibodies, complement, and hepatosplenic sequestration strip an intravenous dose before it reaches a solid tumor, so almost every clinical win is either **injected locally** (intratumoral/intravesical/intraperitoneal) or **rides a carrier** (blood cells, retargeted vector, in-vivo-programmed T cells) rather than surviving the bloodstream. This cluster covers the neutralizing-antibody wall and route-dependence, the bypass strategies (cell carriers, local payload secretion), transgene "arming" (cytokine / BiTE / checkpoint / chemokine-FLT3L), the oncolytic-virus platform landscape by virus family and regulatory status, and the non-viral/retargeted vector frontier (engineered VLPs for transient editor delivery, capsid/envelope-retargeted AAV and lentivirus, in-vivo CAR-T). It mirrors the domain's central pattern — wins **bypass** the delivery wall, they do not break it. Raw source breakdowns live in `sources/`. (16 files)
