# Metabolic Hostility

[[metabolic-hostility]] is the chemical arm of the function gate: the tumor consumes the resources T cells need and floods the space with waste products that disable them. It blocks [[solid-tumor-immune-response]] independently of any suppressive cell.

## Glucose Competition

Tumor cells consume glucose so avidly that they metabolically restrict co-located T cells — dampening mTOR signaling, reducing glycolytic capacity, and lowering interferon-γ production (Chang 2015). Glucose is a limiting resource for which tumor and T cell directly compete, and the tumor usually wins.

## Lactate and Acidosis

High lactate dehydrogenase plus MCT1/MCT4 efflux drive extracellular lactate from a physiological ~2 mM up to **50 mM**, dropping extracellular pH from 7.2–7.4 to **6.4–6.8**. Acidity inhibits T-cell energy metabolism and upregulates inhibitory receptors; high tumor/serum lactate is linked to poor prognosis. Necrotic cells also release potassium, and when intracellular T-cell [K⁺] exceeds a ~40 mM "potassium checkpoint," T cells are functionally suppressed.

## The Adenosine Axis

Dying cells leak ATP, which the ectonucleotidases **CD39** (ATP→AMP) and **CD73** (AMP→adenosine) — expressed by TAMs, MDSCs, Tregs, and exhausted T cells — convert to adenosine. Signaling through the A2A/A2B receptors raises cAMP and impairs T-cell proliferation, motility, and cytotoxicity. This axis is amplified by [[tumor-hypoxia]]. Clinically, single-agent adenosine blockade is inactive, but adding anti-CD73 (oleclumab) to durvalumab in stage-III NSCLC (COAST) raised ORR to 30% versus 17.9% and 12-month PFS to 62.6% versus 33.9% — benefit only in combination and lower tumor burden.

## Amino-Acid Depletion

Tryptophan is catabolized by IDO to kynurenine, which is immunosuppressive and correlates with Treg infiltration. Arginine depletion by arginase arrests the T-cell cycle in G0–G1. Notably, the IDO inhibitor **epacadostat failed cleanly in phase 3** (with pembrolizumab in melanoma, PFS HR 1.00) — the earlier single-arm 55% response rate had reflected pembrolizumab activity, not IDO blockade. It is the cautionary example in this cluster against uncontrolled early-phase readouts.
