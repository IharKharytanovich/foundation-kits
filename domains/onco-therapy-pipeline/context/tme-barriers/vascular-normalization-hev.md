# Vascular Normalization and HEV Induction

[[vascular-normalization]] is the most evidence-backed bypass of the [[tumor-endothelial-barrier]]: rather than destroying tumor vessels (which over-pruning does, worsening hypoxia), it restores their function so immune cells can get in. It is the counterpart, on the immune-entry side, to the delivery-side bypasses.

## Normalization, Not Eradication

Anti-angiogenic therapy given at high anti-vascular doses over-prunes vessels and aggravates hypoperfusion and [[tumor-hypoxia]]. Judicious anti-VEGF dosing instead **normalizes** the vasculature: improved perfusion, increased pericyte coverage, reduced hypoxia, and restored ICAM-1/VCAM-1 adhesion molecules. Clinically, bevacizumab produced measurable normalization in human rectal cancer with drops in interstitial fluid pressure and microvascular density; cediranib in glioblastoma increased oxygenation and prolonged survival specifically in patients whose perfusion rose. Anti-VEGF improves the delivery and function of both CAR-T and endogenous CD8 T cells. The catch is that the "normalization window" is transient and patient-variable — combining anti-Ang2 with anti-VEGF extends it.

## The Real Prize — High Endothelial Venules (Allen 2017)

Normalization alone is a first step; the substantial gain comes from converting tumor vessels into **high endothelial venules (HEVs)** — the specialized MECA-79⁺ portals through which lymphocytes physiologically enter tissue. In genetic mouse models, anti-VEGFR2 + anti-PD-L1 induced HEVs (nearly tripling MECA-79⁺ vessels in a breast model), and lymphocytes were **3- to 10-fold enriched around HEVs** versus other vessels. The switch is **LTβR signaling**: an LTβR agonist doubled-to-quadrupled HEV formation, an antagonist abolished it. Most strikingly, in checkpoint-recalcitrant glioblastoma — where normalization alone did nothing — forcing HEVs via LTβR agonism during antiangiogenic/anti-PD-L1 therapy converted ~15% of vessels to HEVs and reduced tumor burden by more than 60%.

HEV induction is the mechanistic bridge from "excluded" to "inflamed" and a rational adjunct for making cold, CAR-T-resistant tumors accessible.
