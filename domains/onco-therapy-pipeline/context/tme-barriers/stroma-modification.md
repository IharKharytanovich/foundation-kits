# Stroma Modification — The Bypass That Mostly Failed

[[stroma-modification]] is the intuitive attempt to bypass the [[desmoplastic-stroma-barrier]]: if the dense matrix physically excludes drugs and T cells, degrade it. The intuition is sound in mice and mostly wrong in patients — a cautionary cluster of phase-3 failures.

## The Defining Failure — PEGPH20 (Van Cutsem 2020)

Pegvorhyaluronidase alfa (PEGPH20) degrades hyaluronan, and in mice it lowers interstitial fluid pressure within hours. In the phase-3 HALO-301 trial — 492 patients with hyaluronan-**high** metastatic pancreatic cancer, the population most likely to benefit — PEGPH20 added to nab-paclitaxel/gemcitabine gave **median OS 11.2 versus 11.5 months (HR 1.00, p=0.97)** and median PFS 7.1 versus 7.1 months, despite raising ORR from 36% to 47%. Development was discontinued. Worse, PEGPH20 combined with mFOLFIRINOX was actively harmful (OS 7.7 versus 14.4 months).

## Stroma Is Partly Protective (the double-edged lesson)

The deeper reason these strategies fail is that the stroma is not purely a barrier — it partly restrains the tumor. Sonic-hedgehog inhibition (saridegib) had to be stopped early for detriment, because genetic stromal depletion in pancreatic models **accelerated tumor aggressiveness** (Rhim/Özdemir 2014). FAK inhibition (defactinib) paradoxically reduced intratumoral T-cell infiltration. LOXL2 collagen-cross-linking inhibition (simtuzumab) was neutral. Removing the matrix can open channels for metastatic spread and disrupt the balance that was containing the tumor.

## What Still Has Promise

The more measured approaches — TGF-β blockade (TGF-β is the myCAF-driving cytokine) and angiotensin inhibition (losartan normalizes the collagen/hyaluronan matrix and improves distribution preclinically) — normalize rather than destroy the stroma, but none has a phase-3 survival win yet. The lesson for the pipeline: stromal depletion is a double-edged intervention, and the durable gains in TME access have come from [[vascular-normalization]] and from local delivery, not from dismantling the matrix.
