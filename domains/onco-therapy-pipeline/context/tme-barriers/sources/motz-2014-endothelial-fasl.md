# Motz 2014 — The Endothelial FasL Death Barrier

Defines the tumor endothelium as a selective immune barrier that admits regulatory T cells while killing effector CD8 T cells — a foundational mechanism of immune exclusion.

## Design & Methods

Human tumor tissue (ovarian tissue microarrays, multi-cancer), freshly isolated tumor endothelial cells, endothelial killing assays (HMEC-1/HMVEC), and mouse tumor models (ID8-VEGF ovarian, CT26 colon, Renca, B16).

## Quantitative & Mechanistic Results

- **FasL is selectively expressed on tumor vasculature** (CD34⁺ vessels) in many human and mouse tumors and is **absent from normal vasculature**; confirmed on CD45⁻CD31⁺ endothelial cells (ovarian, n=11).
- FasL⁺ vasculature is associated with **scarce CD8 infiltration and a predominance of FoxP3⁺ Tregs** in ovarian, colon, bladder, prostate, and renal cancers — **breast is an exception**.
- **Selectivity mechanism:** endothelial FasL kills effector CD8 and CD4⁺CD25⁻ T cells, but **CD4⁺CD25⁺ Tregs resist** due to higher anti-apoptotic **c-FLIP**; c-FLIP knockdown sensitized Tregs, and transducing c-FLIP into CD8 conferred resistance.
- **Induction cocktail:** tumor-derived VEGF-A + IL-10 + PGE2 cooperatively induce endothelial FasL (in vitro 1 µM PGE2, 50 ng/ml VEGF-A, 10 ng/ml IL-10).
- **Reversal in vivo:** anti-VEGF-A (G6-31) + aspirin/COX inhibition reduced the number and percentage of FasL⁺ vessels across all four mouse models; disrupting Fas–FasL (gld, lpr, or anti-FasL) markedly increased spontaneous CD8 tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes and the CD8/Treg ratio and reduced tumor volume in a CD8-dependent manner.

## Limitations

Breast cancer showed no FasL–CD8 correlation; the Treg-versus-effector selectivity is c-FLIP-dependent and may vary with T-cell activation state.

## Conclusion

The tumor endothelium is an active, selective immune barrier — a FasL "death barrier" that tolerizes the tumor by admitting Tregs and killing effector CD8 T cells. VEGF + COX blockade dismantles it, one mechanistic pillar of the vascular-normalization rationale.
