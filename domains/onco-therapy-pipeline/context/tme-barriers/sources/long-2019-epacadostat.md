# Long 2019 (ECHO-301) — The Definitive IDO Inhibitor Failure

The phase-3 trial that ended the IDO-inhibitor field and stands as the cluster's cautionary example against uncontrolled early-phase readouts.

## Design & Cohort

Phase 3, randomized, double-blind. **706 patients** with unresectable stage III/IV melanoma: 354 to epacadostat (an IDO1 inhibitor) + pembrolizumab versus 352 to placebo + pembrolizumab.

## Quantitative Results

- **Median PFS: 4.7 versus 4.9 months, HR 1.00 (0.83–1.21)** — no benefit.
- **OS HR 1.13 (0.86–1.49)** — no benefit.

## The Lesson

The result directly contradicted the earlier phase-2 signal (a 55% response rate in 54 patients, single-arm), which had reflected pembrolizumab activity rather than IDO blockade. The failure terminated multiple other IDO1-inhibitor programs (including linrodostat in melanoma and head-and-neck cancer).

## Limitations

Tests one IDO1 inhibitor at one dose; does not rule out IDO biology entirely, but definitively rules out this drug adding to checkpoint blockade in melanoma.

## Conclusion

Inhibiting the tryptophan-depletion (IDO/kynurenine) arm of metabolic hostility did not improve outcomes when added to pembrolizumab. Beyond its specific result, ECHO-301 is the canonical warning that a single-arm response rate can reflect the backbone agent, not the experimental one — a discipline the domain applies throughout its provenance grading.
