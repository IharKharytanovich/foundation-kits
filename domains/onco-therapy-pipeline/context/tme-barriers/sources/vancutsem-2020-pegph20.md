# Van Cutsem 2020 (HALO-301) — The PEGPH20 Failure

The phase-3 trial that defined the failure of enzymatic stroma modification, despite enriching for exactly the patients most likely to benefit.

## Design & Cohort

Phase 3, double-blind, placebo-controlled. **492 patients (ITT)** with untreated metastatic **hyaluronan-high** pancreatic adenocarcinoma: 327 to PEGPH20 (pegvorhyaluronidase alfa, which degrades hyaluronan) + nab-paclitaxel + gemcitabine versus 165 to placebo + the same chemotherapy. PEGPH20 3.0 µg/kg + nab-paclitaxel 125 mg/m² + gemcitabine 1000 mg/m².

## Quantitative Results

| Endpoint | PEGPH20 + chemo | Placebo + chemo | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median OS | **11.2 mo** | **11.5 mo** | HR **1.00** (0.80–1.27); **p=0.97** |
| Median PFS | 7.1 mo | 7.1 mo | HR 0.97 (0.75–1.26) |
| ORR | **47%** | **36%** | ratio 1.29 (1.03–1.63) |

- Excess grade ≥3 adverse events on PEGPH20: fatigue 16.0% vs 9.6%; muscle spasms 6.5% vs 0.6%; hyponatremia 8.0% vs 3.8%; historically higher thromboembolism.

## Context — the Broader Ledger

PEGPH20 + mFOLFIRINOX was actively harmful (OS 7.7 vs 14.4 months). Sonic-hedgehog inhibition (saridegib) was stopped early for detriment (stromal depletion accelerated tumor aggressiveness, Rhim/Özdemir 2014); FAK inhibition (defactinib) reduced intratumoral T-cell infiltration; LOXL2 inhibition (simtuzumab) was neutral.

## Conclusion

Despite enriching for hyaluronan-high tumors and raising ORR from 36% to 47%, PEGPH20 did not improve OS or PFS, and development was discontinued. HALO-301 is the defining evidence that enzymatic stromal depletion does not translate to survival — and, together with the SHH and FAK results, that the stroma is partly protective, making its removal a double-edged intervention.
