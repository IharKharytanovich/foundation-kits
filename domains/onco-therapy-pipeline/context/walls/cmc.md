# The CMC / Manufacturing Wall

What actually fails approvals is not efficacy but manufacturability: [[cmc-manufacturing-wall]] — 74% of FDA CRLs (2020–24) are quality/CMC defects. Personal batches are unique, so each needs individual release, sterility, and chain-of-identity (>$100k/patient; AAV producer line 6–12 months; manual CAR-T ~50 steps / ~80 h). It sets the manufacturing throughput ceiling.
