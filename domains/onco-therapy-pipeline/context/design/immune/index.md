# Immune Branch

Computable: somatic variants, HLA, binding/presentation, mRNA construct design (delegated to mrna-design). Empirical: immunogenicity (<60% validate), off-tumor toxicity, persistence/trafficking, response of "cold" tumors. Rate-limited by neoantigen immunogenicity (see [walls/index.md](../../walls/index.md)).
