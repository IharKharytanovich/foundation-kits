# KEYNOTE-942: mRNA-4157/V940 + Pembrolizumab in Resected Melanoma

## Trial Design

KEYNOTE-942 (NCT03897881) is a randomized, open-label Phase 2b trial in patients with resected high-risk stage III/IV melanoma. Patients received either mRNA-4157/V940 (individualized neoantigen mRNA vaccine encoding up to 34 neoantigens per patient) plus pembrolizumab, or pembrolizumab alone (standard-of-care adjuvant).

## Key Efficacy Results

The primary endpoint is recurrence-free survival (RFS):

- **18-month RFS**: combination arm showed a 44% reduction in risk of recurrence or death versus pembrolizumab alone (HR 0.561, 95% CI 0.309–1.017, one-sided p = 0.053). At the pre-specified boundary, this was statistically significant per the trial's alpha-spending plan.
- **3-year RFS update (ASCO 2024)**: sustained benefit with HR ~0.59, corresponding to absolute RFS improvement of approximately 10–12 percentage points at 3 years.
- **Distant metastasis-free survival (DMFS)**: HR 0.347 (95% CI 0.145–0.828), a 65% reduction — suggesting the vaccine effect is strongest at preventing distant spread, not just locoregional recurrence.

## Manufacturing and Composition

- Each patient's vaccine encodes up to **34 neoepitopes** (the highest capacity among individualized cancer vaccines in clinical development).
- Manufacturing turnaround: approximately **6–8 weeks** from tumor biopsy to first dose (Moderna's mRNA manufacturing platform).
- The mRNA is lipid-nanoparticle encapsulated (same platform as mRNA-1273/Spikevax).

## Regulatory Status

- FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation granted April 2023 for adjuvant melanoma.
- Phase 3 trial (V940-001/KEYNOTE-D18) initiated in late 2023, enrolling ~1,089 patients across melanoma, NSCLC, renal cell carcinoma, and bladder cancer.
- First pivotal readout expected 2025–2026.

## Significance for the Pipeline

This is the most advanced individualized neoantigen vaccine program. The 34-neoepitope capacity and the 44% recurrence risk reduction establish the clinical proof-of-concept for the immune branch's fastest personalized loop. The 6–8 week turnaround sets the current manufacturing speed benchmark for mRNA neoantigen vaccines.
