# Bispecific Antibodies and Checkpoint Combinations in Solid Tumors (2024–2025)

## Imdelltra (tarlatamab-eqvs) — BiTE for Solid Tumors

- **Approval**: FDA accelerated approval May 16, 2024, for extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (ES-SCLC) after platinum-based chemotherapy.
- **Trial**: DeLLphi-301 (Phase 2, single-arm).
- **Mechanism**: bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE) targeting DLL3 (delta-like ligand 3, expressed on >80% of SCLC tumors) and CD3 on T cells.
- **ORR**: 40% (97.5% CI 29–52) at the 10 mg dose in 100 patients; 2 complete responses. Median duration of response 9.7 months.
- **Significance**: first BiTE approved for a solid tumor (all prior BiTE/bispecific approvals were hematologic). Validates the BiTE mechanism beyond heme; however, DLL3 is a lineage marker on neuroendocrine tumors — this is antigen-matched, not personalized.

## ICI + Neoantigen Vaccine Combinations

- **Pembrolizumab + mRNA-4157/V940**: KEYNOTE-942 (see companion source). The combination produced HR 0.561 for RFS; the hypothesis is that the vaccine primes neoantigen-specific T cells while pembrolizumab releases the PD-1 brake on the immune response.
- **Atezolizumab + autogene cevumeran (BNT122)**: Phase 1 study in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (Rojas et al., Nature 2023). 8 of 16 patients (50%) generated measurable neoantigen-specific T-cell responses; those 8 responders had significantly longer recurrence-free survival. ctDNA clearance correlated with immunologic response.
- **Pattern**: ICI alone has a ~15–20% ORR ceiling in most solid tumors (excluding MSI-H/dMMR). Adding a personalized vaccine aims to expand the responding T-cell repertoire beyond what checkpoint release alone achieves.

## Bispecific Landscape in Solid Tumors (Beyond Imdelltra)

- **Amivantamab (Rybrevant)**: EGFR × MET bispecific, approved January 2024 for NSCLC with EGFR exon 20 insertions. ORR ~40%. Not personalized — targets a specific shared mutation class.
- **Cadonilimab**: PD-1 × CTLA-4 bispecific, approved in China 2022 for cervical cancer; under global Phase 3 investigation.
- **Tebotelimab**: PD-1 × LAG-3 bispecific, Phase 2 in multiple solid tumors.
- **Emerging T-cell engagers**: CLDN18.2 × CD3 (zolbetuximab-adjacent targets in gastric), GPC3 × CD3 (hepatocellular), MUC16 × CD3 (ovarian) — all Phase 1–2.

## Key Limitation for Personalized Pipeline

BiTE/bispecific therapies are predominantly **matched** (target a shared antigen), not **personalized** (individualized to the patient's tumor mutations). They complement the personalized loop (neoantigen vaccines, TIL) by providing off-the-shelf engager options. The design question for the pipeline is: when does the patient need a bespoke therapy (personalized vaccine, TIL) versus a matched engager? The answer is driven by the antigen landscape of the individual tumor.
