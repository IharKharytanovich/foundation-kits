# TIL and TCR-T Therapy Approvals for Solid Tumors (2024)

## Amtagvi (lifileucel) — First TIL Therapy Approval

- **Approval**: FDA accelerated approval February 16, 2024, for unresectable or metastatic melanoma previously treated with PD-1 and, if BRAF V600+, a BRAF ± MEK inhibitor.
- **Trial**: C-144-01 (single-arm, multicenter).
- **ORR**: 31.4% (95% CI 23.0–40.8) in 153 evaluable patients; 3 complete responses (2.0%). Median duration of response not reached at 18.6 months median follow-up.
- **Manufacturing**: autologous TIL expanded from resected tumor tissue. Manufacturing time approximately **22 days** (range 15–35 days) from tumor excision to infusion-ready product; requires lymphodepleting chemotherapy (fludarabine + cyclophosphamide) before infusion, followed by IL-2.
- **Significance**: first-ever FDA approval for a TIL therapy; validates the concept that patient-derived tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes can be therapeutically expanded and re-infused against solid tumors.

## Tecelra (afamitresgene autoleucel) — First TCR-T Approval

- **Approval**: FDA accelerated approval August 2, 2024, for unresectable or metastatic synovial sarcoma, HLA-A*02:01-positive, MAGE-A4-positive, previously treated.
- **Trial**: SPEARHEAD-1 (single-arm, Phase 2).
- **ORR**: 36.4% (95% CI 22.4–52.2) in 44 patients; 2 complete responses (4.5%). Median duration of response 11.6 months.
- **Target**: engineered autologous T cells expressing a TCR targeting MAGE-A4 (cancer-testis antigen), restricted to HLA-A*02:01 (present in ~40–50% of patients of European descent).
- **Manufacturing**: leukapheresis → genetic engineering → expansion. Manufacturing time approximately **4–6 weeks**.
- **Significance**: first-ever TCR-T approval; demonstrates that engineering a T-cell receptor against a shared antigen (rather than expanding endogenous TILs) can achieve clinical responses in solid tumors. HLA restriction limits the eligible population.

## Manufacturing Speed Evolution

- Traditional TIL manufacturing: 5–7 weeks (2020 era). Iovance's rapid expansion protocol (REP) shortened this to ~22 days.
- Next-generation TIL platforms targeting <14 days: Instil Bio (ITIL-306) and Obsidian Therapeutics exploring gene-modified TIL with cytokine-circuit controls.
- CAR-T manufacturing for heme malignancies: 3–4 weeks (Kymriah, Yescarta); solid-tumor CAR-T remains investigational with no approvals as of 2025.

## Limitations

Both approvals are accelerated (confirmatory trials required). ORRs are modest (31–36%), reflecting the challenge of solid-tumor immunotherapy. Manufacturing is patient-specific, slow, and expensive (>$400k list price for Amtagvi). The immune branch's fastest personalized loop is still the neoantigen vaccine (weeks, not months), not adoptive cell therapy.
