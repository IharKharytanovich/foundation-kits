# Individualized mRNA Neoantigen Vaccine Manufacturing

BioNTech's autogene cevumeran (BNT122) is the most advanced individualized mRNA cancer vaccine in clinical development as of 2025, with data from pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) published in Nature 2023.

## Manufacturing Process

**Cell-free platform.** mRNA manufacturing is cell-free — no cell culture, no bioreactor, no producer cell line. The process is: (1) computational neoantigen prediction from WGS/WES + RNA-seq data; (2) mRNA sequence design encoding up to 20 patient-specific neoantigens in a single construct; (3) in-vitro transcription (IVT) of the mRNA from a linearized DNA template; (4) enzymatic capping and poly(A) tailing; (5) purification (HPLC to remove dsRNA contaminants, tangential flow filtration); (6) LNP formulation via microfluidic mixing; (7) fill-finish and release testing.

**Turnaround time.** BioNTech reported a manufacturing turnaround of approximately 6–9 weeks from tumor biopsy to first dose in the Nature 2023 trial, including the computational pipeline (~2 weeks for sequencing + neoantigen calling) and GMP manufacturing (~4–6 weeks for IVT + formulation + QC/release). The company's stated goal is to reduce total turnaround to under 4 weeks.

**Scalability advantage.** Because the process is cell-free, each patient's product does not occupy a bioreactor for days or require a cleanroom culture suite. The IVT reaction is a bench-scale enzymatic synthesis (~hours, not days). This makes mRNA the only truly N-of-1-scalable manufacturing platform — the same equipment line can produce sequential patient batches in rapid succession.

## Clinical Results (Nature 2023)

In the phase 1 pancreatic cancer trial (N=16 adjuvant patients), 8 of 16 patients (50%) showed neoantigen-specific T cell responses. At median follow-up of 18 months, responders showed significantly longer recurrence-free survival than non-responders. The manufacturing success rate was 100% — all enrolled patients received their individualized vaccine, in contrast to autologous cell therapy where manufacturing failure excludes some patients.

## Cost

Per-patient manufacturing cost for individualized mRNA vaccines is estimated at $15,000–$30,000 — substantially below the $50,000–$100,000 for autologous CAR-T — driven by the cell-free process, shorter manufacturing time, and smaller facility footprint per batch.

## Impact

mRNA represents the only manufacturing platform where N-of-1 personalization does not create a linear manufacturing bottleneck. The cell-free process eliminates the bioreactor dependency and culture-failure risk that constrain viral vector and cell therapy platforms.
