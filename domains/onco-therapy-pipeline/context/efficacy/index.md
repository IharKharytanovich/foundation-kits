# Efficacy — Metrics and the Feedback Loop

Two kinds of metric plus the loop back to diff. Delivery metrics (editing %, %CAR+, expression) and action metrics (RECIST, MRD/ctDNA) are countable — computable. But the link metric → clinical outcome (surrogacy) is empirical and unsettled: ctDNA is prognostic (HR 5–11 for recurrence) but not an accepted OS/DFS surrogate (no trial-level R² >0.7); in-vivo editing measurement in solid tumors remains aspirational (no circulating readout, biopsy is one spatial sample). Resistance/evolution closes the cycle: pre-existing antigen-negative subclones (30–60% of CAR-T relapses, detectable at 0.003–1.8% VAF before treatment) drive relapse and feed a new diff (see [map.md](../map.md)).
