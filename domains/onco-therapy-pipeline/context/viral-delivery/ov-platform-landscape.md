# The Oncolytic-Virus Platform Landscape

[[ov-platform-maturity]] is the state of [[oncolytic-virotherapy]] organized by backbone family. Each family has a distinct tumor-selectivity mechanism and a distinct clinical trajectory; three products are approved and the rest are phase 1/2 or discontinued.

## Herpes Simplex (HSV-1) — the Most Clinically Advanced

Tumor selectivity via ICP34.5/ICP6 deletion (exploiting tumor PKR/interferon defects). A generational ladder: HSV1716 (γ34.5 only) → G207 (+ICP6; pediatric glioma, median OS 12.2 vs ~5.6 months) → **G47Δ/teserpaturev** (+α47; the only OV approved for a primary brain cancer — Japan 2021; phase 2 GBM 1-year survival 84.2% vs 15% threshold) → fusogenic/armed **RP1** (RH018 strain + GALV fusogen + GM-CSF; IGNYTE ORR 32.9%, abscopal in 79% of non-injected lesions, yet FDA Complete Response Letter July 2025 over single-arm design), RP2, T3011, CAN-3110 (uniquely retains γ34.5 under a nestin promoter; seropositive patients survived longer). **T-VEC** (GM-CSF-armed HSV) was the first FDA/EMA approval (2015; OPTiM durable response 16.3% vs 2.1%).

## Adenovirus — Selective and Deliverable

Selectivity via Delta-24/E1A-CR2 deletion (Rb-pathway-dependent replication). **Cretostimogene/CG0070** posted the cycle's best NMIBC data (BOND-003 complete response 75.5%); **nadofaragene firadenovec** (replication-defective Ad-IFNα2b) is FDA-approved for NMIBC (2022). DNX-2401 shows a durable-survival tail in glioma and DIPG. Enadenotucirev is uniquely **blood-stable and IV-deliverable**, the backbone for the BiTE/chemokine-armed NG-641.

## Vaccinia — Repeated Clinical Disappointment

Selectivity via TK/VGF/RR deletion. Every randomized HCC readout was negative: **PHOCUS** phase 3 (pexa-vec) gave median OS 12.7 vs 14.0 months (HR 1.193, futility); TRAVERSE (IV) 4.2 vs 4.4 months, blunted by anti-vaccinia antibodies. ASP9801, TG6002, and TBio-6517 were terminated. BT-001 gave the cleanest mechanistic proof (intratumoral-only anti-CTLA-4) but was halted after phase 1.

## Reovirus and the RNA Viruses — Immune Primers, Route-Sensitive

Reovirus (pelareorep) is naturally RAS-selective, given IV, and works as an immune primer + checkpoint sensitizer (breast IND-213 OS 17.4 vs 10.4 months; myeloma + carfilzomib ORR 54–70%). Measles (CD46), VSV-IFNβ-NIS (Indiana strain, IV, IFNβ-armed), coxsackievirus CVA21 (ICAM-1), poliovirus PVSRIPO (CD155; GBM survival plateau 21% at 24 and 36 months), and Seneca Valley virus (ANTXR1) round out the family — all limited by rapid neutralizing-antibody development and the [[ov-route-dependence]] rule.

## The Recurring Lesson

Small uncontrolled or dose-finding studies generate optimistic signals (Heo 2013 pexa-vec; early CVA21) that **repeatedly fail randomized testing** (PHOCUS, TRAVERSE, N0923, CVA21 STORM/CLEVER). The field's genuine value is combination with checkpoint blockade and the abscopal effect, not single-agent systemic oncolysis.
