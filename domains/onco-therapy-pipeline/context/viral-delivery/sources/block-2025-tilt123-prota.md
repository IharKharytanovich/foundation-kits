# Block 2025 (PROTA) — IL-2/TNFα-Armed Adenovirus in Ovarian Cancer

A peer-reviewed clinical readout for a dual-cytokine-armed oncolytic adenovirus, illustrating both the promise and the small-n, single-arm limits of the armed-OV clinical field.

## Design & Cohort

Phase 1a. **15 patients** with platinum-resistant/refractory ovarian cancer received **TILT-123** (Ad5/3-E2F-D24-hTNFα-IRES-hIL-2, an oncolytic adenovirus co-arming human TNFα and IL-2) via intravenous plus intraperitoneal and/or intratumoral injections, combined with pembrolizumab.

## Quantitative Results

- **Disease control 64% (9/14 evaluable); median PFS 98 days; median OS 190 days.**
- **No dose-limiting toxicities.** Adverse events: fever 40%, fatigue 40%, nausea 40%.
- Response was associated with higher baseline and post-treatment anti-adenovirus neutralizing-antibody titers (an intriguing correlate, echoing the CAN-3110 seropositivity-benefit finding).

## Companion (TILT-123 + TIL, TILT-T215)

In a separate cohort combining TILT-123 with tumor-infiltrating-lymphocyte therapy in 17 checkpoint-resistant melanoma patients: ORR 11.7% (2/17), 1 durable complete response (mucosal melanoma, ~3 years), median OS 620 days. The virus served as an adjuvant to TIL therapy.

## Limitations

Small n; combination design (cannot isolate the virus's contribution); phase 1b (adding pegylated liposomal doxorubicin) ongoing. As with every armed-OV trial, there is no unarmed control arm.

## Conclusion

TILT-123 delivers IL-2 + TNFα intratumorally — cytokines far too toxic to give systemically at active doses — and combined with pembrolizumab produced 64% disease control in a hard platinum-resistant ovarian population. It exemplifies both the local-payload-secretion rationale for cytokine arming and the interpretive ceiling of single-arm armed-OV clinical data.
