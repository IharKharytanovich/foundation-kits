# Demircan 2024 — DART-AAV, CD8-Retargeted Gene Delivery

Demonstrates that a receptor-retargeted AAV can hit a defined immune-cell subset in vivo with near-absolute specificity and liver de-targeting — an AAV-based alternative to lentivirus for in-vivo T-cell engineering.

## Design & Methods

CD8-specific designed ankyrin repeat proteins (DARPins) inserted into the GH2/GH3 loop of VP1 of AAV2 and AAV6 (DART-AAV), with native receptor binding ablated. Tested in donor blood, primary cell mixtures, and systemic injection into conditioned humanized and immunocompetent mice; genomic modification read out via Cre delivery into indicator (reporter) mice.

## Quantitative Results

- **On-target selectivity** (% CD8⁺ among transduced cells): **>98%** (hCD8-AAV2lL, human PBMCs), ~100% (hCD8-AAV6).
- **In vivo:** up to **80% of activated CD8⁺ T cells transduced** after a single vector injection (humanized or immunocompetent mice); humanized-mouse blood specificity 96±3% (AAV2) / 94±4% (AAV6); immunocompetent 99.2±0.8% blood, 90.1±4.1% spleen.
- **Efficiency:** up to 40% of activated human CD8⁺ in blood (NSG); up to 80% of non-activated CD8⁺ in spleen with conditioning.
- **Liver de-targeting:** "exceptional" — mCD8-AAV2lL liver signal hardly distinguishable from control (natural AAV liver tropism suppressed).
- Doses 1.7×10¹¹ vg (humanized), 2×10¹¹ vg (immunocompetent). Capsid core structure unaltered (cryo-EM).

## Limitations

Efficiency depends on T-cell activation state (0.36–0.49% CD8⁺ without conditioning); reporter-based readout of editing; mouse only; no tumor-efficacy endpoint.

## Conclusion

DARPin-displayed, receptor-ablated AAV achieves near-absolute cell-type selectivity for in-vivo immune-cell gene delivery with liver de-targeting — turning the vector's default hepatic biodistribution into a chosen destination. It is a concrete instance of retargeting-as-delivery-bypass and an AAV route toward in-vivo T-cell engineering.
