# Freedman 2017 — EnAd-EpCAM-BiTE, Secretion and Redirection Quantified

The most exhaustively quantified secretion/activation numbers in the BiTE-armed oncolytic-virus literature, and a demonstration that the approach works in genuine patient material.

## Design & Cohort

Enadenotucirev (EnAd; a blood-stable chimeric Ad11p/Ad3 group-B oncolytic adenovirus) engineered to encode an EpCAM×CD3 bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE) under a CMV or splice-acceptor (SA) late promoter. Models: colorectal (DLD-1, HCT-116, SW480) and ovarian (SKOV3) lines; and **primary human malignant ascites / pleural effusions from 12–13 patients** (6 ovarian, 2 mesothelioma, 1 each breast/colorectal/lymphoma/lung).

## Quantitative Results

- **BiTE secretion:** recombinant BiTE ~20 µg/mL at 48 h from transfected HEK293A; from virus-infected DLD cells **165 µg per 10⁶ cells** (CMV promoter) versus 50 µg (SA promoter). Functional **EC50 = 7.4 ng/mL**.
- **T-cell activation** (endogenous ascites T cells): dual CD25⁺/CD69⁺ rose to **50–95%** (donor-dependent); BiTE-mediated activation 67.6% versus 58.9% for CD3/CD28 beads.
- **T-cell proliferation:** up to **30-fold** expansion in ascites samples.
- **Killing:** SKOV3 lysis within 16 h (CMV-BiTE) versus 44 h (SA-BiTE); EpCAM⁺ target depletion in **all** patient samples across effector:target ratios of 0.13–37.6 — despite T-cell PD-1 ~65% (up to 93%) and immunosuppressive IL-10 88–633 pg/mL.
- Viral dose 100 vp/cell; genome replication equivalent to parental.
- **Delta vs unarmed EnAd:** unarmed virus produced no BiTE and no T-cell activation/killing; oncolysis was identical.

## Limitations

Ex-vivo / patient-sample driven (no autologous in-vivo tumor model); no survival endpoint.

## Conclusion

An oncolytic virus can secrete enough functional BiTE to activate a patient's own T cells and clear tumor targets even in an immunosuppressive ascites microenvironment, adding an entire T-cell-redirection axis on top of unchanged oncolysis. This is the biology underpinning the clinical T-SIGn/NG-641 platform.
