# Andtbacka 2021 (CALM) — Intratumoral CVA21 Works

The local-route arm of the cleanest natural experiment in oncolytic-virus route-dependence: the same virus is active injected intratumorally and inert given intravenously.

## Design & Cohort

Phase II, single-arm. **57 patients** with stage IIIC–IVM1c advanced melanoma received **intratumoral coxsackievirus A21 (CVA21/Cavatak/V937)** up to 3×10⁸ TCID₅₀ per injection. CVA21 is an ICAM-1-tropic wild-type enterovirus.

## Quantitative Results

- **Primary endpoint met:** 6-month immune-related progression-free survival **38.6% (21/57)**; median immune-PFS 4.2 months.
- **ORR 28.1%** (2015 abstract) to **39%** (final paper, different assessment windows); **durable response rate 19.3%**; **1-year survival 75.4%**.
- No grade 3/4 product-related adverse events.
- Immune correlates: CD8⁺ and PD-L1 upregulation in 5/6 biopsies; immune-infiltrate reconstitution in checkpoint-failed lesions; Th1/interferon gene shift.

## The Route Contrast (context)

- **Intratumoral CVA21 + pembrolizumab (CAPRA):** ORR 47%, CR 22%, median OS 30.9 months.
- **Intravenous CVA21 (STORM/KEYNOTE-200):** ORR 6% (monotherapy) to 20% (urothelial), no better than pembrolizumab alone, with 86% of intratumoral virus undetectable by day 15.
- **Intravenous CVA21 + ipilimumab (CLEVER, uveal melanoma):** ORR 0%.

## Limitations

Single-arm; ORR figures vary by assessment window; Merck discontinued the V937 program in October 2022 (portfolio prioritization).

## Conclusion

Intratumoral CVA21 produces genuine responses and abscopal immune activation, while the same virus given intravenously fails — the empirical core of oncolytic-virus route-dependence. For a tumor a needle can reach, inject it; naked IV virus does not survive the systemic-delivery gauntlet.
