# Yu 2014 — EphA2-TEA-VV, the Clean In-Vivo BiTE Survival Delta

The clearest paired in-vivo demonstration that BiTE arming, not oncolysis, produces cures — a jump from no cures to 100% complete remission versus the unarmed backbone.

## Design & Cohort

Double-deleted vaccinia (vvDD, Western Reserve, TK⁻/VGF⁻) secreting an EphA2×CD3 BiTE. Model: A549 lung cancer; SCID / SCID-Beige mice reconstituted with human PBMCs. Viral dose 1×10⁸ pfu intraperitoneally; tumor 2×10⁶ A549 + 1×10⁷ PBMCs subcutaneously; EphA2-TEA-VV n=13 vs GFP-VV n=12.

## Quantitative Results

- **In-vitro:** replication and oncolysis equal to unarmed GFP-VV; A549 killed dose-dependently by both viruses, but 15% of tumor cells remained viable at the highest MOI (5) at 96 h without T cells — adding T cells to EphA2-TEA-VV significantly increased killing (p<0.05).
- **T-cell activation:** only EphA2-TEA-VV (not GFP-VV) activated PBMCs — IL-2 ~1 ng/mL plus IFN-γ — and induced bystander killing of non-infected A549.
- **In-vivo (key delta):** EphA2-TEA-VV produced **complete remissions in all treated animals** in the subcutaneous A549 model; tumor size p<0.001, survival p<0.0001 versus GFP-VV. T-cell tumor infiltration (day 5) occurred only with the armed virus.
- **Delta vs unarmed GFP-VV:** unarmed virus + T cells did not cure; armed virus gave 100% complete remission. Oncolysis/replication itself was unchanged by arming.

## Limitations

Xenograft + human PBMCs (no intact immune system); single antigen (EphA2) and cell line.

## Conclusion

Arming an oncolytic vaccinia with a T-cell engager converts a virus that lyses but does not cure into one that produces universal complete remission by redirecting T cells — the cleanest in-vivo evidence that the armed-vs-unarmed benefit is T-cell-mediated. Foundation for the vaccinia-BiTE and adenovirus-BiTE programs.
