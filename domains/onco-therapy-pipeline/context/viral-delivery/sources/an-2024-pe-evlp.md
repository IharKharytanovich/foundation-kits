# An 2024 — Prime-Editor eVLPs

Extends transient virus-like-particle delivery to prime editors — the largest, hardest-to-deliver editor class — closing a gap that AAV cannot serve because prime-editor machinery exceeds a single AAV's cargo limit.

## Design & Methods

Virus-like particles packaging **prime-editor protein + pegRNA + nicking sgRNA** as transient ribonucleoprotein. Systematic engineering of v3/v3b architectures. Models: human cells plus two mouse genetic-blindness (retina) models.

## Quantitative Results

- v3 and v3b PE-eVLPs achieved **65- to 170-fold higher prime-editing efficiency in human cells** than the earlier base-editor-derived PE-eVLP construct.
- In vivo, single injections of v3 PE-eVLPs produced "therapeutically relevant levels" of prime editing in the retina, with protein-expression restoration and partial visual-function rescue in two blindness models.

## Limitations

Exact in-vivo tissue percentages and doses are in the figures/main text rather than the abstract; prime editing is intrinsically lower-efficiency than base editing; the in-vivo demonstration is retina-only; short follow-up.

## Conclusion

Transient VLP delivery scales to prime editors (~6–8 kb-scale machinery) that exceed a single AAV's ~4.7 kb capacity — so the transient-RNP approach is not just safer on off-target but also the practical way to deliver the biggest editors in one particle. Together with the base-editor eVLP work, it establishes the engineered-VLP platform across the full editor size range.
