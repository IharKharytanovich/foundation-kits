# Todo 2022 — G47Δ, the Only OV Approved for Brain Cancer

The pivotal readout behind the first (and only) oncolytic virus approved for a primary brain cancer — and a cautionary example of how survival numbers depend on the measurement anchor.

## Design & Cohort

Single-arm, single-institution phase 2. **19 patients** with residual or recurrent glioblastoma after radiotherapy received repeated stereotactic intratumoral **G47Δ** (a triple-mutated HSV-1: both γ34.5 copies deleted + ICP6 inactivated + α47 deleted; the α47 deletion de-represses US11 and improves MHC-I antigen presentation) at 1×10⁹ pfu, up to 6 doses.

## Quantitative Results

- **Primary endpoint — 1-year survival rate 84.2%** (95% CI 60.4–96.6) versus a pre-specified 15% historical threshold — decisively met; the trial was terminated early at interim for efficacy.
- **Median OS 20.2 months from G47Δ initiation** (95% CI 16.8–23.6); **28.8 months from initial diagnostic surgery**; median PFS 4.7 months.
- Responses: 1 partial response (5.3%), 18 stable disease, 0 complete response — survival benefit **without striking radiographic shrinkage** (an immunologic response pattern).
- Biopsies showed increased tumor-infiltrating CD4/CD8 T cells and persistently low Foxp3⁺ Tregs.
- Safety: fever 89.5%, vomiting 57.9%, nausea 52.6%, lymphocytopenia 47.4%.

## Limitations

Small, single-arm, single-institution, historical-control comparison; MRI pseudoprogression frequent. Crucially, the **28.8-month figure is anchored to initial surgery** while recurrence-anchored trials measure from a later point — direct cross-trial comparison overstates G47Δ's apparent advantage. The companion phase I/II (Nat Commun 2022) reported 1-year survival 38.5% from last dose in a progressive-GBM cohort — the same virus, a different baseline.

## Conclusion

G47Δ met its 1-year-survival endpoint decisively and became the only oncolytic virus approved for a primary brain cancer (Japan, conditional 7-year approval, June 2021). The immunologic (not radiographic) response pattern and the measurement-anchor caveat are the key interpretive points for the knowledge base.
