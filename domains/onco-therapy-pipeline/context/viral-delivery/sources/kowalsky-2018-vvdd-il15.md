# Kowalsky 2018 — IL-15-Superagonist Vaccinia: 80% Cured vs 0% Unarmed

The reference paired armed-vs-unarmed experiment for cytokine arming: the cure-rate delta is enormous, the CD8-dependence is absolute, and in-vitro oncolysis is unchanged — the template for how arming works.

## Design & Cohort

vvDD (Western-Reserve double-deleted vaccinia) armed with an IL-15/IL-15Rα superagonist fusion (vvDD-IL15-Rα) versus **unarmed vvDD**. Models: MC38 colon and ID8 ovarian peritoneal carcinomatosis; 2.0×10⁸ pfu intraperitoneally; n=5–10 per group; with and without anti-PD-1 and with CD8/CD4/NK depletion.

## Quantitative Results

- **Cure rate (MC38):** **80% long-term cured (armed) versus tumor progression at days 23–30 (unarmed)**; significant tumor-mass reduction versus vvDD from day 9.
- **CD8 infiltration:** rose from ~10% (PBS) to ~30% for **both** vvDD and armed virus — i.e. the infiltration increase is oncolysis-driven, not cytokine-specific — but tumor-specific IFN-γ ELISPOT was "dramatically enhanced" in the armed arm (functional, not just numeric).
- **Payload:** the IL-15/IL-15Rα fusion measured 747 pg/mL in vitro (armed) versus non-detectable (unarmed).
- **Checkpoint synergy:** adding anti-PD-1 gave **100% survival past 200 days** (p≤0.001 vs any other group).
- **CD8-dependence:** CD8 depletion cut median survival to 47 days (versus >150 days); CD4/NK depletion had minimal effect. Cured mice resisted MC38 rechallenge (durable memory).

## Limitations

2018 (predates the 2023–2026 window, but remains the reference IL-15-superagonist OV — no stronger paired 2023–26 dataset exists); peritoneal models; the CD8-percentage increase itself is not superior to unarmed — the cure rate and functional/quality difference are the deltas.

## Conclusion

Cytokine arming's benefit is immune-mediated and CD8-dependent: the armed virus lyses identically to the unarmed one but cures 80% versus 0% by generating functional, memory anti-tumor CD8 immunity, amplified to 100% cure with checkpoint blockade. This is the quantitative archetype for the armored-oncolytic-virus thesis.
