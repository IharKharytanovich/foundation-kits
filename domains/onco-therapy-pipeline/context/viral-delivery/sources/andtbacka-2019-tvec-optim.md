# Andtbacka (OPTiM) — T-VEC, the First Approved Oncolytic Virus

The only randomized controlled dataset with a true comparator arm in the oncolytic-virus field, and the efficacy floor for GM-CSF-armed oncolytic HSV.

## Design & Cohort

Randomized, open-label phase 3. Unresectable stage IIIB–IV melanoma. **T-VEC (n=295)** — a GM-CSF-armed, ICP34.5/ICP47-deleted HSV-1, injected intratumorally (≤4 mL of 10⁶ then 10⁸ PFU/mL every 2 weeks) — versus **subcutaneous GM-CSF (n=141)**.

## Quantitative Results

- **Durable response rate (≥6 months): 16.3% (48/295) vs 2.1% (3/141)** GM-CSF (OR 8.9; p<0.001). (The final analyses report DRR 19.0% vs 1.4%.)
- **ORR 26.4% vs 5.7%; complete response 10.8% vs <1%** (final: CR 16.9% (50 patients) vs 0.7% (1)).
- **Median OS 23.3 vs 18.9 months** (HR 0.79; p=0.051, near-significant / p=0.0494 descriptive in final analysis).
- **Abscopal / lesion-level (quantitative proof of systemic effect from local injection):** injected lesions reduced ≥50% in 64%; **34% of non-injected non-visceral lesions and 15% of visceral lesions reduced ≥50%.**
- 5-year OS among complete responders 88.5%.

## Limitations

OS was not formally significant on the primary boundary; benefit concentrated in earlier-stage (IIIB–IVM1a) and treatment-naïve patients; T-VEC is injected intratumorally (not systemically).

## Conclusion

T-VEC became the first FDA/EMA-approved oncolytic virus (2015) on the strength of a durable response rate 8-fold higher than GM-CSF, and its lesion-level data quantify the abscopal effect — 34% of non-injected lesions regressing — that underpins the whole intratumoral-injection-plus-immunity strategy. As the only randomized comparator dataset, it is the clinical efficacy floor against which armed next-generation HSVs (RP1, T3011) are read.
