# Nicolai 2024 — VivoVec In-Vivo CAR-T in Primates

The strongest peer-reviewed evidence that CAR-T cells can be generated inside an immunocompetent primate from a single injection, without lymphodepletion — the translational bridge to the human in-vivo-CAR-T trials.

## Design & Cohort

**4 immunocompetent pigtailed macaques (Macaca nemestrina)**, no lymphodepletion. A third-generation self-inactivating lentivirus, **cocal-pseudotyped**, displaying a **multidomain fusion (MDF) ligand incorporating CD58 + anti-CD3 scFv + CD80** costimulation, carrying an **anti-CD20 CAR**. Intranodal administration (up to 4 lymph nodes, ≤1 mL/node). Three animals at standard dose, one at 5× lower.

## Quantitative Results

- **Peak up to 65% of circulating T cells CAR⁺ at day 10**; absolute CAR⁺ counts 596 / 3,875 / 11,182 cells/µL across three responding animals.
- **Complete B-cell elimination by day 7**, sustained undetectable through **56, 63, and 76 days** (>10 weeks aplasia).
- One animal showed CAR-T re-expansion at day 49 (memory) coinciding with B-cell return.
- The **5-fold lower-dose animal showed minimal response** (dose threshold).
- Biodistribution (day 139): transgene only in injected inguinal + downstream iliac node; minimal spleen/marrow/lung; **no hepatic transduction**.
- The CD80/CD58 MDF enhanced binding/activation/transduction versus anti-CD3 scFv alone.
- Transient CRS-like signals (mild tremors / brief seizure ~day 10) managed with tocilizumab + anakinra.

## Limitations

n=4, single species; TU/vg dose not in main text; B cells are a surrogate (no tumor); intranodal (not IV) delivery; transient neurotoxicity.

## Conclusion

First peer-reviewed proof that in-vivo CAR-T works in an immunocompetent primate without lymphodepletion, with durable deep B-cell aplasia and no hepatic transduction — the highest-confidence in-vivo-CAR-T dataset, superseding company press-release claims. It grounds in-vivo CAR-T as a double bypass of both the manufacturing wall and the solid-tumor delivery wall.
