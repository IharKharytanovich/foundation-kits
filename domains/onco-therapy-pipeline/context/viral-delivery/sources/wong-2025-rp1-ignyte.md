# Wong 2025 (IGNYTE) — RP1 + Nivolumab in Melanoma

The strongest peer-reviewed systemic-disease dataset for a next-generation oncolytic HSV — and the center of the field's central regulatory tension between strong response data and the FDA's demand for randomized evidence.

## Design & Cohort

Phase 1/2, single-arm, open-label, registration-directed cohort. **140 patients** with anti-PD-1-**failed** advanced cutaneous melanoma (65.7% primary anti-PD-1 resistant, 56.4% PD-L1-negative, 46.4% prior both anti-PD-1 + anti-CTLA-4). RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec; strain RH018A HSV-1, ICP34.5/ICP47-deleted, armed with GM-CSF + the fusogenic GALV-GP-R⁻ glycoprotein) intratumorally — first dose 1×10⁶ PFU/mL, then up to 7 doses at 1×10⁷ PFU/mL every 2 weeks (≤10 mL) — plus nivolumab.

## Quantitative Results

- **ORR 33.6% (mRECIST, BICR; 25.8–42.0)**; CR 15.0%, PR 18.6%; RECIST 1.1 sensitivity ORR 32.9%.
- **Median DOR 33.7 months** (14.1–NR); median OS not reached (non-responders 18.5 months); **1-year OS 75.3%, 2-year OS 63.3%**.
- **Abscopal (key strength):** ≥30% reduction in **93.6% (73/78) of injected** and **79.0% (94/119) of non-injected lesions** — including visceral — with similar depth/durability.
- Immune (day 43 vs baseline): CD8⁺ infiltration up in 47.4% (37/78), PD-L1 up in 57.4% (39/68).
- Safety: grade 3/4 TRAE 12.9%; no grade 5 / no treatment-related deaths.

## Limitations & Regulatory Status

Single-arm, no control, heterogeneous population — the exact basis of the **FDA Complete Response Letter (July 2025)**, which stated IGNYTE was "not an adequate and well-controlled investigation" and "cannot be adequately interpreted due to heterogeneity of the patient population." Company/trade-press figures (3-year OS, median PFS 30.6 months) are not in the JCO paper and should not be cited as peer-reviewed. Confirmatory randomized phase 3 IGNYTE-3 (NCT06264180, OS endpoint) is ongoing.

## Conclusion

RP1 + nivolumab produced strong, durable, systemic (abscopal) responses in a checkpoint-refractory melanoma population where anti-PD-1 alone yields <10% — the fusogenic GALV glycoprotein being the plausible driver of the deep abscopal effect. Yet the single-arm design left it unapproved, making IGNYTE the emblematic case of the oncolytic-virus field's efficacy-versus-evidence tension.
