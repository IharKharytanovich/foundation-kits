# Cell-Carrier Delivery — Hitchhiking Past the Antibody Wall

[[ov-cell-carrier-delivery]] is the bypass that turns the [[ov-neutralizing-antibody]] wall from fatal into survivable for intravenous virotherapy. In mice, pre-existing NAbs block IV oncolytic virus outright; in humans, the virus loads onto circulating blood cells that shield it and carry it to tumor.

## The Human Proof (Adair 2012)

In a window-of-opportunity trial, 10 colorectal-cancer patients with liver metastases received a single cycle of intravenous reovirus before surgical resection. All 10 were neutralizing-antibody-positive at baseline, yet reovirus protein was found by immunohistochemistry in **9 of 10** resected tumors (greatest in the metastases, essentially absent from normal liver), and **replication-competent virus was recovered from fresh tumor in all 4 patients** with available tissue — and from no normal liver. The mechanism was explicit in the blood: reovirus genome appeared in plasma and in the PBMC, granulocyte, and platelet fractions, but **viable, cytotoxic virus was recovered only from the blood cells, not from plasma**. The cells carried live virus for 1 hour to 5 days post-infusion; plasma did not.

## Internalization Is the Shield (Ilett 2011; Ilett/Berkeley 2018)

Electron microscopy showed dendritic cells **internalize** reovirus while T cells retain it on the surface; DC-loaded reovirus killed melanoma even in human neutralizing serum, where free virus was blocked. The strongest statement came from Ilett/Berkeley 2018: human monocytes take up **pre-formed reovirus–antibody complexes via Fcγ receptors (FcγRIII/CD16)** and still deliver functional, replicative virus to tumor cells — so antibody binding does not inactivate the therapy and can even facilitate carrier uptake.

## Engineered Carrier Cells — Deliberately Loaded Trojan Horses

Beyond natural blood-cell hitchhiking, cells can be **deliberately pre-loaded** with virus ex vivo and re-infused. The definitive naked-vs-carrier experiment (Mader 2009): in measles-immune mice, naked virus gave no survival benefit (31 days, same as saline) while MSC-delivered virus doubled survival to 66 days (p<0.0001), with ~32-fold neutralizing-antibody protection measured directly. The logic reaches the clinic:

- **Mesenchymal stromal cells (CELYVIR).** Autologous MSCs carrying an oncolytic adenovirus were safe over repeated infusions (no grade 2–5 toxicity), with adenovirus detectable in blood in 7/9 pediatric patients — though >50% of enrollees screen-failed during the ~6-week MSC-expansion window.
- **Neural stem cells (glioma).** NSC-delivered CRAd-S-pk7 into the resection cavity of newly-diagnosed glioma reached median OS 18.4 months (18.0 in the MGMT-unmethylated subset, versus ~12–14 historical) with the MTD not reached.
- **CIK and T cells.** CIK cells deliver oncolytic vaccinia (the extracellular-enveloped form is 3% of inoculum but ~36% of tumor-delivered virus; >50% complete responses in MC38); TCR-transgenic T cells raise the tolerated VSV dose >4-fold and the intratumoral titer ~10-fold over naked virus.

The unifying number: carrier delivery buys ~15–32× antibody protection, and in-situ amplification turns the tiny transferable load (often <1% of the carrier's virus) into >10⁶ TCID₅₀/mg intratumoral titers. It is why NAb magnitude does not track efficacy across the reovirus myeloma and pancreatic datasets — the virus never has to survive naked in plasma.
