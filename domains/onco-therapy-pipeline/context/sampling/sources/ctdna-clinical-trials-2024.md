# ctDNA-Guided Therapy Trials 2022–2025

## DYNAMIC (Stage II Colon Cancer)

Tie J, Cohen JD, Lahouel K, et al. Circulating Tumor DNA Analysis Guiding Adjuvant Therapy in Stage 2 Colon Cancer. N Engl J Med. 2022;386(24):2261-2272. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2200075.

- Randomized, ctDNA-guided vs standard-of-care adjuvant chemotherapy decision in stage II colon cancer.
- ctDNA-guided arm reduced chemotherapy use by 50% with non-inferior recurrence-free survival (RFS).
- ctDNA positivity rate post-surgery: ~8–10%.
- ctDNA-positive patients without chemotherapy: 3-year RFS ~47% vs ~70% with chemotherapy.
- ctDNA-negative patients (spared chemotherapy): 5-year RFS ~93% (2024 follow-up, ASCO presentation).
- DYNAMIC-III (stage III, NCT05174169): recruiting as of 2024.

## GALAXY / CIRCULATE-Japan (Stage II–III CRC)

Kotani D, Oki E, Nakamura Y, et al. Molecular residual disease and efficacy of adjuvant chemotherapy in stage II-III colorectal cancer. Nat Med. 2023;29(1):127-134. DOI: 10.1038/s41591-022-02115-4.

- n=1,039 patients.
- ctDNA-positive patients who received chemotherapy: 6-month DFS 84% vs 34% observation (HR 0.15).
- ctDNA clearance with chemotherapy: 68% vs 7% observation.
- ctDNA negativity post-surgery HR 10.0 for recurrence (95% CI 5.9–17.0).

## TRACERx 421 (NSCLC)

Abbosh C, Frankell A, et al. Tracking the Evolution of Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer. N Engl J Med. 2023;389(22):2049-2060. DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2307592.

- n=421 patients, phylogenetic-informed bespoke panels.
- ctDNA detected in 49% of patients with relapse.
- Median lead time ctDNA positivity to clinical recurrence: 212 days.
- Specificity of ctDNA negativity: >99% (<1% of ctDNA-negative patients relapsed in surveillance window).
- Extended TRACERx Evo (2024–2025) tracking subclonal dynamics under therapy pressure.

## MRD Detection — Quantitative Landmarks

- Signatera (Natera): tumor-informed 16-variant panel, LOD 0.01% per locus, effective ~0.001% integrated (Reinert 2019 JAMA Oncol, DOI: 10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.0528).
- Breast cancer MRD: ctDNA positivity at 1 year post-surgery HR 25.1 (4.4–130.5); median lead time to relapse 10.7 months (Garcia-Murillas 2019 JAMA Oncol, DOI: 10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.0838).
- PhasED-Seq: LOD ~1 ppm via phased variant enrichment (Kurtz 2021 Nat Biotechnol, DOI: 10.1038/s41587-021-00981-w).
