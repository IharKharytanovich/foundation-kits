# Tumor-Wide, Public Splicing Neoantigens

A pipeline addressing the core weakness of SNV neoantigens — intratumoral heterogeneity — by finding neojunctions expressed *uniformly within a tumor* ("tumor-wide") and *recurrently across patients* ("public"). Peer-reviewed in Nature; the digest below draws on the openly archived preprint (flag: preprint-level detail; some exact counts live only in the Nature figures).

## Design & Method

Neojunction detection with intratumoral-conservation filtering (multi-region sampling) across diverse cancer types (glioma-centric); prediction of neojunction-encoded peptides (NEJs); immunopeptidomic support; TCR discovery and TCR-engineered CD8⁺ T-cell functional testing.

## Results

- Identified neojunctions that recur across patients, some **tumor-wide (spatially/temporally uniform).**
- Two exemplar neopeptide-encoding neojunctions highlighted: **GNAS (tumor-wide) and RPL22 (conserved).**
- Isolated **CD8⁺ T-cell clones** specific for these; **TCR-engineered CD8⁺ T cells conferred neoantigen-specific tumor-cell eradication.**
- Recurrent neojunction expression traced to **cancer-specific splicing-factor dysregulation.**

(Exact neojunction counts/recurrence percentages are in the Nature figures and were not retrievable here — not asserted.)

## Limitations

Public/tumor-wide neojunctions are a subset of all neojunctions; immunogenicity shown for exemplars, not at scale. Significance: a subset of neojunctions are simultaneously intratumorally conserved and public — a molecular basis for **off-the-shelf** T-cell therapies robust to heterogeneity, directly relevant to the manufacturing/personalization bottleneck.
