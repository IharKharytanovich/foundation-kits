# Local Delivery Strategies

[[local-delivery-strategies]] represent the most direct bypass of the [[solid-tumor-delivery]] wall: instead of trying to get a nanoparticle through the bloodstream into a tumor (0.7% ID), inject the payload directly into or adjacent to the target tissue.

bypasses:: [[solid-tumor-delivery]]

Local injection bypasses the entire systemic biodistribution cascade — MPS clearance, liver first-pass, tumor vascular extravasation, interstitial transport — by placing the LNP at 100–1000× higher local concentration than achievable via IV administration.

## Preclinical Evidence

**Intratumoral LNP-mRNA immunostimulation** achieved 83–86% complete regression in syngeneic mouse solid tumor models (Hamouda 2024). The mechanism is local immune activation: LNP-encapsulated mRNA encoding cytokines (IL-12, IL-27, GM-CSF) is taken up by tumor-resident antigen-presenting cells, converting the immunosuppressive TME into an inflamed state that recruits and activates cytotoxic T cells.

**Abscopal effects** extend local treatment to distant tumors: in bilateral tumor models, intratumoral injection of one tumor led to regression or growth arrest of the untreated contralateral tumor in 40–50% of animals. Combined with systemic anti-PD-1, abscopal response rates exceeded 60%.

## Clinical Translation

**Moderna mRNA-2752** (intratumoral OX40L/IL-23/IL-36γ LNP-mRNA) entered Phase 1/2 for accessible solid tumors and lymphoma. Early data showed local tumor regression and evidence of systemic immune activation.

**Image-guided injection** expands the addressable tumor population beyond superficial lesions: CT-guided, ultrasound-guided, and endoscopic injection routes make liver, lung, and pancreatic tumors accessible to local delivery, though with more variable distribution than subcutaneous models.

## Limitations

**Not all tumors are accessible.** Disseminated metastases, diffuse brain tumors, and peritoneal carcinomatosis cannot be individually injected. Local delivery is a strategy for defined, accessible lesions — not a universal solution.

**Heterogeneous intratumoral distribution.** A single injection site does not guarantee uniform LNP distribution throughout a large or irregularly shaped tumor. Multi-point injection protocols and convection-enhanced delivery (CED) address this partially.

**Repeat access.** While LNP itself is redosable (no anti-capsid immunity), repeated intratumoral injections require repeated invasive procedures.

## Position in the Bypass Landscape

Local delivery is one of three bypass routes in the 2024–26 delivery landscape — alongside liver targeting (NTLA-2001, aiming at a hepatic target instead of a solid tumor) and blood-cell targeting (in-vivo CAR-T, programming circulating T cells). Each exploits a different reachable compartment rather than trying to penetrate the solid tumor microenvironment directly.
