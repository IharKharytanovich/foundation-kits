# Delivery-Cascade Barriers

Between "injected" and "edited the cell," each step loses orders of magnitude. [[endosomal-escape]] is the universal physical ceiling: even once a particle is internalized, 98–99% degrades in the lysosome. [[in-vivo-genetic-editing]] of the tumor's own cells — as opposed to the reachable liver — remains preclinical; there is no quantitative in-tumor editing efficiency yet.
