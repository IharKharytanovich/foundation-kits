# Wins Bypass the Wall, They Do Not Break It

[[delivery-bypass]] is the recurring shape of every 2024–26 success: aim at the liver (NTLA-2001, baby KJ), inject locally/intratumorally (83–86% complete regressions in mice), or hit blood T-cells (in-vivo CAR-T, DARPin-LNP ~90% expression in CD8+). None of these penetrate a solid tumor in vivo — they choose targets that avoid needing to.
