# Tombácz 2021 — Anti-CD4 mRNA-LNP T-Cell Targeting

Quantifies the highest in-vivo T-cell recombination of the antibody-conjugated-LNP series, establishing CD4 as an efficient homing marker.

## Design & Model

Anti-CD4 antibody conjugated to an mRNA-LNP via SATA–maleimide chemistry (~3.5 antibodies per 88 nm LNP), carrying reporter/Cre mRNA. Model: Ai6 Cre/loxP reporter mice (C57BL/6), n=8–9 (dose), 6 (kinetics).

## Quantitative Results

- In vitro: ~80% of CD4⁺ cells ZsGreen⁺ at the lowest dose.
- In vivo (spleen): ~60% of splenic CD4⁺ T cells recombined; ~40% in lymph nodes.
- Selectivity: splenic uptake 3.5-fold higher (%ID/g) versus IgG; luciferase 33-fold higher in CD3⁺ splenic cells; localization ratio 6-fold over IgG control.
- Doses 3 / 10 / 30 µg mRNA per mouse (0.4 mg/kg at 10 µg).

## Limitations

Mouse-only; anti-CD4 targets all CD4 T cells plus some myeloid; transient mRNA.

## Conclusion

Anti-CD4-conjugated mRNA-LNPs achieve ~60% in-vivo splenic CD4 T-cell recombination with 33-fold selectivity — the strongest in-vivo T-cell-targeting efficiency of the antibody-LNP papers, complementing the anti-CD5 CAR-T and anti-CD3/CD7 results in defining marker choice for LNP-based in-vivo cell engineering.
