# Parhiz 2018 — Anti-PECAM-1 LNP Endothelial Retargeting

The foundational antibody-directed endothelial-retargeting result and the quantitative de-targeting benchmark echoed by later SORT and targeted-LNP work.

## Design & Model

Anti-PECAM-1 antibody (~80 molecules per particle, SATA–maleimide conjugation) attached to an mRNA-LNP to redirect it to pulmonary vascular endothelium. Confirmed in ApoE-knockout mice to establish ApoE-independence.

## Quantitative Results

- **~200-fold** increase in mRNA delivery and **~25-fold** increase in reporter protein expression in lung versus untargeted LNP (the "two orders of magnitude").
- Lung uptake 105 ± 3.5 %ID/g at 30 minutes (16-fold over IgG-LNP); **lung-to-liver ratio ~200-fold** higher than untargeted.
- Mechanism confirmed ApoE-independent (works in ApoE-knockout mice) — the antibody interaction, not the ApoE corona, drives delivery.

## Limitations

Reporter/protein-expression endpoint (not editing); endothelium-wide, not disease-selective; older ionizable chemistry; mouse-only.

## Conclusion

Antibody conjugation can redirect an mRNA-LNP away from the liver-default to pulmonary endothelium with a ~200-fold delivery gain, independently of the ApoE corona — the foundational proof that an affinity ligand outcompetes ApoE-LDLR, and the quantitative de-targeting benchmark for the ligand-conjugated-LNP field.
