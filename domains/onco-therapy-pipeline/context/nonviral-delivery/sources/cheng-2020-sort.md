# Cheng 2020 — The Founding SORT Paper

Establishes that LNP organ tropism is programmable by a single charged excipient — the field's foundational extrahepatic-delivery principle.

## Design

Add a fifth "SORT" lipid to an optimized four-component base LNP (5A2-SC8 / DOPE / cholesterol / DMG-PEG). The SORT lipid's charge class reprograms the protein corona and predictably redirects tropism. Mouse studies with luciferase, hEPO, IL-10, Cre, and Cas9 mRNA/sgRNA and Cas9 RNP.

## Quantitative Results

- **Charge rule:** base LNP → liver; permanently cationic (DOTAP, DDAB, EPC) ~10–15% → spleen and ~50% → lung; anionic (18PA, 14PA, 18BMP) 10–40% → spleen exclusively; ionizable (DODAP) ~20% → liver enhanced >10×.
- **Expression shift:** titrating DOTAP 0→50% moved luciferase progressively liver → spleen → lung; anionic 18PA gave completely selective spleen delivery with no expression elsewhere.
- **Cell-type transfection (Cre mRNA ~0.3 mg/kg):** liver ~93% of hepatocytes; lung ~40% epithelial + ~65% endothelial; spleen ~12% B cells, ~10% T cells.
- **CRISPR editing (Cas9 mRNA + sgRNA ~2.5 mg/kg):** lung SORT (50% DOTAP) ~15.1% indels; liver SORT (20% DODAP) ~13.9% indels (PTEN); PCSK9 editing in liver ~60% indels → near-complete serum protein knockdown over 3 doses. Cas9/sgRNA RNP co-delivery also demonstrated.

## Limitations

Mouse-only; high-DOTAP lung formulations carry dose-dependent toxicity; primate translation unproven in this paper.

## Conclusion

Organ tropism is programmable by one charged lipid — the founding demonstration that a charge-based corona rewrite defeats the LNP liver-default and enables extrahepatic mRNA delivery and CRISPR editing.
