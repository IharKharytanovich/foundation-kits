# Non-Viral & Next-Generation Delivery — Beyond the Liver-Default LNP

The platform frontier for getting a payload past the delivery wall without a replicating virus. Every systemic lipid nanoparticle defaults to the liver (ApoE→LDLR, ~90% of dose), so this cluster holds the strategies that redirect or route around that default: **charge-based organ retargeting** (SORT lipids dial tropism to lung or spleen), **ligand-conjugated LNPs** (antibody/DARPin-decorated particles that program T cells, hematopoietic stem cells, or endothelium — the LNP route to in-vivo CAR-T), **biological carriers** (CD47-shielded exosomes, cell-membrane-cloaked nanoparticles, and hypoxia-seeking engineered bacteria that colonize the tumor core drugs can't reach), and **physical/local delivery** (focused ultrasound + microbubbles opening the blood-brain barrier, and injectable hydrogel depots that place immunostimulants directly in the tumor). Each is scored honestly: the strongest quantitative wins are extrahepatic organs, immune cells, and local depots — direct systemic solid-tumor-parenchyma targeting remains the least mature, and several headline platforms (iExosomes, membrane-coated NPs, hydrogel immunotherapy) are clinically unproven or preclinical. This cluster extends the domain's [delivery-bypass](../delivery/bypass.md) thesis: the wins route around the wall, they do not break it. Raw per-paper breakdowns in `sources/`.
